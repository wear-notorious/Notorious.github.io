SHIPPING
& RETURNS

Everything you need to know about your NOTORIOUS™ order, from checkout to your doorstep.

01 — WHERE WE DELIVER

Morocco.

NOTORIOUS™ currently accepts and delivers orders across Morocco.

02 — PAYMENT

Cash on Delivery.

No online payment is required. Simply place your order and pay Cash on Delivery when your package arrives. The price shown on our website is the final amount you pay — delivery is free, with no additional fees.

03 — DELIVERY TIME

Made for you.

Our products are prepared after your order is placed. Most orders are expected to arrive within approximately 2–5 business days, depending on production, your city and the delivery service.

04 — SHIPPING FEES

Based on your city.

Enjoy FREE DELIVERY on your NOTORIOUS™ order. The price displayed on our website is the final amount you pay upon delivery. No additional delivery fees.

05 — CHOOSE YOUR SIZE

Check before you order.

Every product page includes a Size Guide. Please check your measurements carefully before placing your order because our products are prepared specifically for each order.

06 — WRONG OR DAMAGED ITEM

We’ll make it right.

If you receive an incorrect, damaged or defective product, contact NOTORIOUS™ as soon as possible and provide clear photos of the item and the issue so your case can be reviewed.

IMPORTANT

Please check your order before confirming it.

Make sure your size, phone number, city and delivery address are correct. Accurate information helps us prepare and deliver your NOTORIOUS™ order as smoothly as possible.
